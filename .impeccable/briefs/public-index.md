# Surface brief — public/index.html (landing page)

Scope: the public landing page; privacy.html and account-deletion.html inherit its world in Read mode.
Mode: Persuade. Audience: a Thanaweya Amma student on their phone, arriving from a shared link.
Belief to land first (user, 2026-09-25): "you will learn to study the right thing."
Action: download the APK. Constraints: Arabic first; app palette, radii and Readex Pro pinned by the user; no invented proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is a corrected exam answer sheet (ورقة إجابة متصححة). It refuses the app-landing default of phone mockup, feature cards and stat row.
OWN-WORLD: the app's grey desk (#EFEFF2 / black) with white answer sheets (radius 22). Black Readex Pro ink. Red pen (#FF3B30) for marks, crosses and Ruqaa margin notes; green (#34C759) ticks; ballpoint blue (#1B6EF3) only for the student's own writing and the one action. Ruled lines, a margin rule, a boxed score, an «تعليمات» box. No shadows, depth by value only.
STORY: a student sees a paper being graded, understands the grade comes from real answers, sees which chapter the red pen circles, and downloads.
FIRST VIEWPORT: one tilted answer sheet fills the phone width. Header fields (name / subject); boxed score top-left, which the red pen circles on load. Tagline as the answer, in heavy ink. A Ruqaa note pointing at the weak lesson. Blue download button at the sheet's foot.
FORM: exam answer sheet, candidate 1 of 7 in the ordered list; user-selected on the question round. No seed key (launcher not installed).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
