التطبيق: أثر (com.ather.app) · الناشر: [[PUBLISHER_NAME]]

الطريقة الأسرع: من داخل التطبيق

  1. افتح أثر.
  2. دوس على أيقونة الإعدادات (⚙) فوق على اليمين.
  3. انزل لكارت حذف الحساب.
  4. دوس حذف الحساب، وبعدها امسح حسابي نهائيًا.
ده مش بيتراجع. كل امتحان سجّلته هيروح — من التليفون ومن السحابة. مفيش نسخة تانية عندنا نرجّعها لك.

Delete your Ather account

App: Ather (com.ather.app) · Publisher: [[PUBLISHER_NAME]]

Fastest way: in the app

  1. Open Ather.
  2. Tap the settings icon (⚙) at the top.
  3. Scroll to the Delete account card.
  4. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account permanently.
This cannot be undone. Every exam you have recorded will be gone — from the phone and from the cloud. We hold no other copy to restore for you.

If you cannot open the app

Email us from the address registered on your account at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] with "Delete my account" as the subject. We ask you to send from the registered address because that is what confirms the account is yours.

Accounts are deleted within 30 days of the request.

Exactly what is deleted

DataDeleted?
Your account (email, display name, account identifier)Yes, immediately
Your cloud copy (chapters, lessons, exams)Yes, and before the account itself
Your study record on the phoneYes
Any other copy anywhereThere is none — Android's automatic backup is switched off for Ather

There is no retention period after deletion and no archival copy.
